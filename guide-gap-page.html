Before you choose: the one number all four depend on

Every route in this guide turns on the same two figures. What the property is worth today, and what is actually owing against it.

Not what you paid for it. Not the assessment. Not what sold on the street two years ago. Values have moved, and a plan built on an old number is a plan built on nothing.

  • Adding a suite or a second unit only makes sense measured against what the property is worth now.
  • Releasing equity depends entirely on how much of it there actually is.
  • And a sale only helps if what is left, after the mortgage and the costs of selling, is a number worth having.

Most people reading this are working from a figure that is two or three years old. That is usually the difference between a plan that holds and one that falls apart in the second week.
