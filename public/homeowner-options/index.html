The paths between struggling and selling.

A calm, plain-language menu of the options most homeowners are never walked through. Not all will fit your situation — the point is to see that the choices are wider than they first appear.

01

Lower what the home costs you

Restructuring, lender hardship options, property-tax and insurance moves that ease the monthly pressure.

02

Have the property earn its keep

Legal suites, renting a room or parking, mid-term furnished rentals — space you already have, working for you.

03

Reverse mortgage, severing surplus land, partners, sale-and-lease-back, rent-to-own — cash or breathing room while you stay.

04

Legalizing a basement suite, targeted high-return improvements, and clearing the issues that scare lenders.
