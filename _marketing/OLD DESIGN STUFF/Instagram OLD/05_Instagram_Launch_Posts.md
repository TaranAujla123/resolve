## POST 2 — Power of Sale & Mortgage Arrears

**Image card hook:**
A Notice of Sale is not the end of the story.

**Caption:**
A few facts about power of sale in Ontario that most homeowners do not realize until far too late.

1. Power of sale is not foreclosure. The lender recovers what they are owed; any equity above that still belongs to you.

2. The redemption period typically gives you weeks to act, not days. Your lawyer will confirm the exact dates that apply to your file.

3. A homeowner-led sale, run carefully, almost always produces a stronger net result than a lender-led sale — better presentation, more time, more buyers, the lender communicated with rather than dictating.

4. Doing nothing is the most expensive option on the table.

The earlier the file is handled, the more options exist. A private consultation costs nothing.

## POST 2B — Mortgage Arrears *(new split from Power of Sale)*

**Image card hook:**
Missed payments are not the same as lost equity.

**Caption:**
Falling behind on a mortgage in Ontario is more common right now than most homeowners realize. The first instinct — silence, panic, or a quick "we buy houses" cash-offer call — is almost always the worst move.

A few things worth knowing.

1. The lender is required to communicate before any sale process starts. The window for negotiation is real.

2. Refinancing, restructuring, or a private bridge are often viable options before a power of sale ever begins.

3. If selling becomes the right answer, a properly run homeowner-led sale almost always nets more than waiting for the lender to take over.

4. The earlier the conversation happens, the more paths stay open.
