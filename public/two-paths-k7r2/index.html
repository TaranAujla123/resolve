The Resolve Coordinated Sale

What a lender can rely on when the owners sell with us.

Nothing is paused

We never ask you to delay a step. Your rights, your timetable and your full claim for any balance stay exactly as they are.

You decide

Every offer goes to you the day it arrives. No offer that leaves a balance owing becomes firm without your written consent.

Fair market value

Priced from comparable sales and an independent appraisal, with written price reviews. Your appraiser is welcome.

A weekly record

A written activity report every Monday, with third-party proof: views, showings, feedback and offers.

Vacant possession

The owners commit in writing to leave on closing, with a move-out plan we track every week.

Confidential

The terms of any discharge stay between you, the owners and their counsel. Nothing is published, and no lender is ever named.
